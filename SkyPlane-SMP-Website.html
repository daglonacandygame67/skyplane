What the server is

SkyPlane SMP is one big world with fifteen cities based on real ones: Lion City, Tokyo, London, New York, Dubai and ten more. Each city has its own style of houses and landmarks, an airport, a metro, a mall and a theme park. There's plain Minecraft countryside between them, so you can still go off and build a base in the Wild.

You get a free flat in Lion City when you first join. From there you can get a job, save up for a bigger place, open a shop in the mall, or learn to fly and start an airline with your friends. Planes, trains and cars all use the resource pack the server sends you, so you don't need any mods.

It works on Java and on Bedrock (phone, Xbox, Switch, PlayStation, Windows), and it's free.

Update 7

Cities got a lot bigger

November 2026
  • New skyscrapers in every city, in six different shapes. Some take up a whole block, and some are twin towers joined by sky bridges. Each floor has its own flat layout.
  • Housing estates on the edge of each city: terraced houses, semi-detached houses, bungalows and big houses with pools. All of them are furnished and for sale.
  • Every city now has a theme park with two roller coasters you can actually ride, plus a Ferris wheel, carousel, drop tower, bumper cars and a haunted house.
  • Every city also has a three-storey mall with about 30 shops for sale, a food court and a cinema.
  • Every building with more than one floor has a lift. Stand on the pad and pick your floor.
  • You can lock your door with a code: /doorcode set 1234. Three wrong guesses set off an alarm and text you.
  • Streets and paths have more lights, so cities are properly lit at night.
  • /menu puts everything in one place. There's also a sidebar showing your money and job (hide it with /hud), player warps and /rtp.
  • New features: graves for your items when you die, /skin, map art from images, chopping down whole trees and vein mining.
  • The voice chat site has been rebuilt. Voices get quieter with distance and come from the side the speaker is standing on. Messages you send there also pop up on the other player's in-game phone.

The fifteen cities

The cities sit on a 5 × 3 grid about 6.5 km apart. Bullet trains run between them, and every city has at least one airport. You can also take a SkyAir hop from /menu if you don't want to fly yourself.

What you can do

Getting around

  • 24 aircraft, from gliders and helicopters up to jumbo jets, with fuel, flaps, gear and runway slots from ATC
  • Driverless metro, a monorail and bullet trains, with departure boards
  • 14 cars, city buses every few minutes, boats, ferries and a cruise ship

Homes and land

  • Buy a flat, house, villa or shop in any city, or rent one out
  • Claim land in the Wild to protect your base
  • Door codes, smart locks, doorbells and cameras

Money

  • Jobs: pilot, train driver, courier, taxi, firefighter, paramedic, chef and more
  • A bank with savings and loans, stocks, an auction house and chest shops
  • Daily rewards and daily tasks

Things to do

  • Roller coasters and fairground rides in every city
  • Parkour, boat races, elytra rings, fishing contests and an arcade
  • Fireworks at 21:00, night markets, festivals and mayor elections

Friends

  • Proximity voice chat in your browser, no mod needed (type /voice in game)
  • A phone with maps, messages, the bank, a flight radar and the news
  • Clubs, emotes, selfies, marriage and leaderboards

Fair play

  • City land is protected, and everything is logged so griefing can be rolled back
  • Police, a court and a jail for people who break the rules
  • Anti-cheat, plus graves so you don't lose your items when you die

How to join

Java Edition

  1. Open Multiplayer and press Add Server.
  2. Paste the address below.
  3. When it asks about the server resource pack, choose Yes.
  4. Once you're in, type /menu.

Best on 26.3. Older versions can join too, but some models may look off.

  1. Go to Servers, scroll down and press Add Server.

Rules

  1. Be decent to people. No harassment, slurs or threats. Players of all ages play here.
  2. No hacked clients, X-ray, fly or speed hacks, auto-clickers or abusing bugs.
  3. Don't grief in cities. Land there belongs to whoever owns it, and we can roll back anything.
  4. The Wild has no rules about fighting and raiding. Claim your land if you want it protected.
  5. Don't park on runways or railway tracks, and don't crash vehicles into builds on purpose.
  6. No trading for real money, no scamming in the auction house, and no alt accounts farming rewards.
  7. Don't advertise other servers.
  8. If staff make a decision you disagree with, appeal it calmly. Don't argue about it in chat.
